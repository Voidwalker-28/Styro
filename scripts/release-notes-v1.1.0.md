# Styro v1.1.0

Styro is now a **real Android launcher** — not just a preview.

## What's new

- **Set as default launcher** — Android now offers Styro as a home app. A new "Make Styro your home app" card in Onboarding and Native Capabilities explains what changes, then requests the system role. You can switch back any time in system Settings.
- **Real installed apps** — App Space now lists your actual installed apps with their real icons, instead of the demo catalog. Your favorites, folders, categories, and hidden apps are preserved across the switch.
- **Real launching** — tapping an app opens it. No more "would open here."
- **New app icon** — the geometric Styro mark in white on black.
- **Cleaner permissions** — removed system permissions the app doesn't use.

## Still honest

Notification badges, wallpaper reading, and real widgets remain clearly labeled as needing future native work. Styro never fakes a system action.

## Install

Download **Styro-v1.1.0.apk** below and install it on your Android device. Upgrades from v1.0.0 keep your settings.
